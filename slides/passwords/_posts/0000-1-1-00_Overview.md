By following a few basic guidelines, we can:  <!-- .element: class="r-fit-text" -->  
 - Significantly reduce our risk of falling victim to cyber-attacks  
 - Ensure that our data remains secure  
 - Our online activities are safe  

---
Cyber Hygiene Guidelines:   <!-- .element: class="r-fit-text" -->
- Use Strong Passwords &rarr; Passphrases, Passkeys, MFA, and Password Managers  
- Use Anti-Malware and Host Based Firewalls   
- Regularly Update Software  
- Use encryption  
- Backup data  
- Lock devices  
- Recognize Phishing  

---

 - non-contiguous character types and cases
 - a mix of letters, numbers and other characters
 - change your password every 90 days
