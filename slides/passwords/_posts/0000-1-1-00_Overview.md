If we don't keep clean what might happen?  <!-- .element: class="r-fit-text" -->
 - Physical Effects
     - Body odor - caused by the breakdown of sweat by bacteria
     - Possible skin infections or conditions - buildup of dirt and dead skin cells
     - Dental problems - plaque buildup, leading to gum disease, cavities and tooth decay and bad breath.
     - Illness - Without regular washing we may contract infectious diseases
 - Mental and Social Effects
     - Social Isolation
     - Impacts Employment
     - Low Self-Esteem and Confidence

---
How does this have to do with Cybersecurity Awareness?

When it comes to being online, our niavety or even bad habits can cause major security issues.  
This campaign is to help make all of us aware that we need go have good habits, or good hygiene when it comes to being online.

This is called Cyber Hygienics - Like personal hygiene, these are routine and consistent practices that protect against digital threats
