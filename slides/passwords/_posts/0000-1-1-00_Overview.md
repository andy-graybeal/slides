What does this mean?
Adopting a security-centric mindset and habits that help individuals and organizations mitigate potential online breaches. 
A fundamental principle of cyber hygiene is that it becomes part of everyday routine.


---
Basic Cyber Hygiene Practices:  
- Use Strong Passwords, Passkeys, Passphrases, MFA, and Password Managers
- Anti-Malware and Host Based Firewalls
- Regularly Update Software
- Use encryption
- Backup data
- Lock devices
- Recognize Phishing
- Secure mobile device
- Secure IoT devices

## Strong Passwords 

Before August 2025
We were to use complicated passwords with:

 - non-contiguous character types and cases
 - a mix of letters, numbers and other characters
 - change your password every 90 days
   
This practice has been retired.  
Why do you think this was retired?
