 - Physical Effects
     - Body odor - caused by the breakdown of sweat by bacteria
     - Possible skin infections or conditions - buildup of dirt and dead skin cells
     - Dental problems - plaque buildup, leading to gum disease, cavities and tooth decay and bad breath.
     - Illness - Without regular washing we may contract infectious diseases
 - Mental and Social Effects
     - Social Isolation
     - Impacts Employment
     - Low Self-Esteem and Confidence

Cyber hygiene Definition:
The steps that users of computers and other devices can take to improve their online security and maintain system health. 

---

What does this mean?
Adopting a security-centric mindset and habits that help individuals and organizations mitigate potential online breaches. 
A fundamental principle of cyber hygiene is that it becomes part of everyday routine.


---
Basic Cyber Hygiene Practices:  
- Use Strong Passwords, Passkeys, Passphrases, MFA, and Password Managers
- Anti-Malware and Host Based Firewalls
- Regularly Update Software
- Use encryption
- Backup data
- Lock devices
- Recognize Phishing
- Secure mobile device
- Secure IoT devices
